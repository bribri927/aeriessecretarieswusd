Connecting Your Virtual Classroom

Syncing your external digital platforms with Aeries eliminates manual scoring, but requires precise configuration to prevent grade book discrepancies. **Please Note: WUSD supports Direct Grade Passback only for Canvas.** Google Classroom acts as a roster and assignment import tool (no automated grade push back). Let's explore how to safely map, sync, and troubleshoot your integrations!

WUSD Direct Integration Policy Notice

To protect student data integrity and avoid synchronization locks, WUSD enforces two strict integration behaviors:

  • Canvas Grade Passback is Supported: You can push finalized grades directly from Canvas to Aeries. Categories (such as Homework, Tests) must match spelling and case exactly.
  • NO Google Classroom Grade Passback: WUSD does *not* authorize Google to write grades back into Aeries. You can import student rosters and pull assignments *into* Aeries, but grading calculations must be completed or synced through manually configured columns.

LMS Protocol • Canvas Direct Sync

Setting Up Canvas Category Mapping & Passback

Canvas allows you to push assignment grades directly to Aeries with a single click. For passback to succeed, your Canvas Assignment Groups must map **identically** to your Aeries Gradebook Categories.

Aeries: Manage Gradebook → Categories → Canvas Category Mapping (Or LMS Sync)

The Canvas Passback Setup Flow:

  1. In **Aeries**, configure your grading categories (e.g., *Tests*, *Homework*). Note spelling and capitalization.
  2. In **Canvas**, navigate to your Assignments page and create **Assignment Groups** with matching names (e.g., if Aeries says "Homework", Canvas must say "Homework" — not "HW" or "homework").
  3. Enable the toggle **"Sync to SIS"** on individual Canvas assignments. Unchecked assignments will be skipped during passback.
  4. In Canvas Gradebook, click **Actions → Sync to SIS** (or direct LTI export) to push marks to your Aeries spreadsheet.

LMS Protocol • Google Classroom Limitations

Google Classroom Roster Import & Setup

While WUSD prohibits automated grade passback from Google to protect records from background writes, you can safely pull student rosters and assignment details *from* Google Classroom into Aeries.

Open Gradebook → Manage Gradebook → Add/Link Sections → Link Google Classroom

How to Link & Pull Roster Data:

  1. Open the target Aeries gradebook. Select **Add/Link Sections** (or Google Connection tab).
  2. Click **Link Google Classroom**. Select your district G-Suite account to authorize single sign-on (SSO).
  3. Aeries will display your active class sections. Click **Import Students** to match your Aeries roster to your Google Classroom list.
  4. To pull homework names, select **Import Assignments**. This automatically creates matching grading columns inside Aeries, leaving grades empty for manual or import scoring.

Sync Management • Data Integrity

Resolving Duplicate Grades & Sync Disconnects

A common sync issue occurs when a single Canvas assignment exports twice, creating duplicate columns inside Aeries. Let's look at how to resolve this.

  • Duplicate Columns: If an assignment creates duplicate rows, verify if you changed the assignment name in Canvas *after* the initial passback ran. Aeries treats changed names as new entries. Delete the older column in Aeries and run the passback again.
  • Missing Canvas Students: If a student's grades are blank after passback, check if their email address matches across systems. If they are missing their district email in Canvas, the sync bypasses them.
  • Re-Authorizing Connections: If the sync fails with an authorization error, navigate to **Manage Gradebook → LMS Options**, click **Disconnect**, and immediately **Re-Authorize** to refresh security tokens.

Interactive Tools • Supplemental Sync

Configuring Quizizz & Edpuzzle Syncing

Game-based or supplemental programs (such as Quizizz, Blooket, and Edpuzzle) do not sync directly to Aeries database tables. They must be routed through Canvas or Google Classroom.

The Dual-Step Sync Routing:

  1. Link your Quizizz or Edpuzzle account to your **Canvas Course** (or Google Classroom).
  2. Assign the quiz directly inside the Canvas shell as an external tool assignment.
  3. Once students complete the activity, let Quizizz sync the scores directly to your Canvas gradebook.
  4. Run the standard **Canvas SIS Passback** to pull the finalized scores into Aeries.
