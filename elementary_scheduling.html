How to Build Your Elementary Master Schedule

This is a live learning guide. Instead of just reading, you will be building. Open this workspace page in one window on your monitor, and open your active Aeries database in another window alongside it. We will proceed chronologically to configure and audit your database structures from scratch before scheduling a single student.

Your Active Build Strategy

Building an elementary schedule requires absolute precision. We are going to treat every step as a live design session. By entering sections and constraints sequentially, you prevent system logic errors during the student loading phase. Let's align your database before making changes.

Our Goal:

By the end of this guide, you will have configured terms, balanced school sizing counts, mapped out teacher FTE constraints, and organized clean student requests in your real database window.

The Live Checklist We Are Creating

  • Phase 1: Align calendar deadlines and school day hours.
  • Phase 2: Match class sizing counts with our staffing.
  • Phase 3: Build structural sections (singletons first!).
  • Phase 4: Resolve duplicate or broken requests.

Understanding the Aeries Tables We are Modifying

As you work inside Aeries, you are directly interacting with specific database tables. Let's look at the three primary tables we will be configuring and auditing together:

STU (Student Data Table)

Stores who the students are. This includes their base demographic information, grade level, and enrollment status.

SSS (Student Requests Table)

Stores what classes they want. This lists the specific course codes requested by families or counselors.

MST / SMS (Master Schedule Table)

Stores where/when classes occur. This houses the physical structures: teachers, periods, rooms, and seat caps.

The 5 Beginner Training Milestones

Work through these five steps systematically. Do not skip steps. Let's begin.

Step 1 — Set Up Self-Contained Classrooms

Beginner Milestone 1 • Base Class Logic

Goal: Configure students to be assigned to a single, main classroom teacher for the bulk of their instructional day.

Aeries Path (Find this in your Aeries Window):

School Info / Scheduling → Master Schedule (or Scheduling Master Schedule)

What we are doing together:

  1. Create a single, main section for each self-contained teacher.
  2. Assign the teacher's unique ID to that section.
  3. Set the course code to represent "Homeroom" or "Grade Level" block courses.
  4. Ensure the attendance flag is enabled for this section so it acts as your primary attendance-taking hub.
Teacher Tip: In elementary scheduling, you don't need a separate section for Math, Science, and Social Studies unless your district specifically tracks grades at the course level. Using a single "Homeroom" or "Elementary Block" section simplifies grading and attendance.

Step 2 — Configure AM/PM Kindergarten Sections

Beginner Milestone 2 • Half-Day Cohorts

Goal: Safely split half-day students into balanced morning and afternoon shifts in Aeries to ensure accurate reporting and clean attendance tracking.

Aeries Path (Find this in your Aeries Window):

School Info / Scheduling → Master Schedule → Section Details

What we are doing together:

  1. Create two distinct sections for each kindergarten teacher in the Master Schedule.
  2. Assign Section 1 (AM) to Period 1 and Section 2 (PM) to Period 2 (or use your district’s specific Kindergarten Period codes).
  3. Set the seat cap (MST.MX) for both sections to match district limits (e.g., 20 students per section).
  4. Audit each section to ensure correct AM/PM flags are populated.
Beginner Mistake: Putting both AM and PM kindergarten students into the same section number. If you do this, Aeries will mark PM students absent during AM attendance calls! They must be separated into unique section numbers.

Step 3 — Map Specialist Rotations (PE, Music, RSP)

Beginner Milestone 3 • Specialist Alignment

Goal: Ensure teachers receive their contractually obligated prep minutes while their classes are supervised by PE and Music specialists.

Try out this Specialist Prep Minute Auditor:

Weekly Prep Minutes Provided: 100 Minutes

What we are doing together:

  1. Identify specialist blocks in Aeries (e.g., Music, PE, Art).
  2. Create secondary sections where the PE/Music teacher is assigned as the primary teacher of record.
  3. Check that the times of these sections match exactly when the home-room teacher expects their prep periods.

Step 4 — Build Multi-Grade Combo Classes

Beginner Milestone 4 • Split Grade Levels

Goal: Build and enroll multi-grade split sections (e.g., a 2nd/3rd grade combo class) without breaking student transcripts or grade levels.

Aeries Path (Find this in your Aeries Window):

Scheduling → Master Schedule → Sections

What we are doing together:

  1. Do not create two separate physical classes for the same room. Instead, create a single section.
  2. Assign the teacher to this section.
  3. When enrolling students, populate the section using their true grade level codes in the `STU.GR` field.
  4. Ensure the seat capacity accommodates both grade levels combined without exceeding class caps.
Best Practice for Combos: Make sure you coordinate with your site testing coordinator early. State testing runs by grade level, so students in split combo sections must be sorted correctly into their respective testing windows.

Step 5 — Establish Intervention Support (RSP, ELD)

Beginner Milestone 5 • Support Services Mapping

Goal: Configure Aeries so push-in or pull-out intervention staff can easily track support sessions without disrupting the student's primary self-contained attendance.

Aeries Path (Find this in your Aeries Window):

School Info / Scheduling → Courses / Supplemental Services

What we are doing together:

  1. Assign the student to their primary self-contained classroom section for core morning attendance.
  2. Use supplemental scheduling tabs or non-attendance tracking codes to associate students with RSP or ELD teachers.
  3. Do not map these pull-out services as primary attendance sections. Doing so will generate double-booked class flags on validation reports.
