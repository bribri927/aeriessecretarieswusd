Flex Scheduling Models

How To Add Flex, WIN, Advisory, or Block Scheduling

Use this page to clarify purpose, attendance, supervision, student assignment, and program conflicts before adding flex scheduling.

Before You Add Flex/WIN/Advisory

  • Purpose defined: intervention, enrichment, advisory, support, enrichment, SEL, or office hours.
  • Attendance process decided.
  • Student assignment process decided.
  • Supervision plan confirmed.
  • SPED/ELD/intervention conflicts reviewed.
  • Bell schedule minutes verified.

How To Add Flex Scheduling

Step 1 — Name the purpose

Do not start with the period. Start with why the time exists.

Examples: WIN intervention, advisory, enrichment, academic support, intervention rotation.

Step 2 — Decide who assigns students

Principal, counselor, teacher, system rule, student choice, or rotating schedule.

Step 3 — Decide how attendance works

Clarify whether attendance is taken in the flex period, advisory class, assigned teacher, or another process.

Step 4 — Protect service minutes

Check that flex does not unintentionally block SPED, ELD, counseling, intervention, or required minutes.

Step 5 — Add flex sections or assignments

Use consistent naming so staff can recognize the period and purpose.

Step 6 — Test with sample students

Check a few students from each grade/program to confirm the schedule makes sense.
