Common flex models

WIN / Intervention

Students receive targeted support or enrichment during a protected block.

Define how students are assigned, who supervises them, and how attendance is taken.

Advisory + Flex

Students begin in advisory, then move to support, enrichment, or intervention based on need.

Without a clear movement and supervision process, this can become operationally messy.

A/B Block

Courses meet on alternating days or longer blocks. Works well for deeper learning but requires careful minutes review.

Traditional 6–7 Period

Simpler to manage and familiar to staff, but may leave less space for intervention or enrichment.

Hybrid Model

Combines traditional periods with embedded flex/advisory days.

Elementary Flex/WIN

Usually grade-level based and built around intervention, specialists, and push-in/pull-out services.

Flex planning questions

  • What is the purpose of flex?
  • How are students assigned?
  • Who takes attendance?
  • Who supervises students?
  • How often do assignments change?
  • What happens if a student does not report?
  • How do SPED, EL, intervention, and counseling services fit?
