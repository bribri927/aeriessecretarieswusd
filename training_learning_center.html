Operational Lifecycle Reminders

Standard Year-Round Operational Milestones

Office tasks fall into predictable calendar patterns. Follow this baseline cyclical reminder sequence to protect site counts and keep clean records throughout the school year.

Beginning of Year

BOY Cleanups

  • Mark opening day **No-Shows** and drop schedules.
  • Audit TK/K immunization yellow cards.
  • Review boundary residency verification forms.
Mid-Year Check

Semester Rotations

  • Verify second-semester drops and add dates.
  • Perform unlinked emergency contact sweeps.
  • Re-assess chronic absenteeism and send Letter 2 warnings.
End of Year

EOY Transcripts & Cum Files

  • Print, verify, and stamp official final transcripts.
  • Reconcile independent study hours with grading blocks.
  • Archive and lock cumulative files for summer transfer.
