Operational Triage • Back-to-school

Symptom: My Gradebook is Completely Empty

Usually, this happens on your first week of school or right after a trimester rollover. You log in, but your dashboard shows zero gradebooks, or last year’s courses.

Why it happens & How to Fix it:

  • Check Your Year Filter context: Aeries defaults your login to the most recent historical year you actively modified. Look at the very top-right of your screen. If it says last year, switch the dropdown to the active academic year.
  • Rosters are Not Loaded Yet: If you are in the correct year, you may need to import your scheduling blocks. Navigate to Manage Gradebook → Add/Link Sections and click Add Sections to pull your classes from the central master scheduling directory.
2
Canvas Integration • Grade Passback

Symptom: Canvas Sync is Running but Grades Are Missing

You initiated Canvas "Sync to SIS" last night, but your grading columns inside Aeries are still empty or completely blank.

Verify the 3 Passback Constraints:

  1. Check Category Name Spelling: Canvas Assignment Groups must match Aeries Categories exactly (including spaces and capitalization). If Aeries says "Homework" but Canvas says "HW" or "homework", the API passback rejects the transfer.
  2. Enable 'Sync to SIS' Toggle: Inside Canvas, click on individual assignments and ensure the "Sync to SIS" checkbox is checked. Unchecked assignments are bypassed.
  3. Verify Student Email Formats: If a single student's grades are missing, check their email in Canvas. It must match their official `@wusd.k12.ca.us` district SSO account.
3
Roster Alignment • Enrollment delays

Symptom: Student is physically present but missing in Aeries

A new student walks into your classroom with a scheduling slip from the office, but their name does not display on your digital attendance or grading rosters.

  • Rosters Sync Overnights: Demographics are entered by school registrars and overnight synchronization runs at 3:00 AM. Allow 24 hours for new enrollments to populate teacher dashboards automatically.
  • Force Student Import: If you need to grade or mark attendance immediately, open your gradebook. Navigate to Manage Students and click Add Students. Aeries will instantly pull any newly registered student from the master scheduling database.
