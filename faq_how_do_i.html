Aeries Classroom FAQ Desk

Let’s take the guesswork out of daily data management. This desk-side index answers high-frequency, hands-on questions about grading scales, co-teacher configurations, and roster synchronization. Open this guide tiled next to your active database window to resolve classroom roadblocks in seconds!

When a student is sick, transfers in mid-semester, or is exempt from a specific activity, use the standard database exclusion flag to bypass grade math entirely. Do not leave the cell blank or key in a numeric value.

How to Do This:
  1. Open your active **Gradebook Spreadsheet** card and locate the target student and assignment row.
  2. Double-click the score cell.
  3. Instead of typing a numeric value, key in the uppercase code EX.
  4. Press **Enter** to save. The cell will display as "EX" and will be omitted entirely from current term score calculations.
⚠️ Common Mistake: Entering a zero score or a blank field. A blank field can cause syllabus calculation alerts, and entering a zero will count as a failing mark, dropping the student's average.
Affected Tables: STU, GRD

If a student fails to submit homework or an exam, entering a plain "0" represents the mathematical score, but typing the district's standard missing code triggers active communication.

How to Do This:
  1. Open the active **Gradebook** card and select the target score cell.
  2. Type the uppercase code MI.
  3. Press **Enter** to save.
  4. Aeries will mathematically process this as a **0% score**, but will display a red "Missing" flag on student and parent portal dashboards—prompting them to submit the outstanding work.
💡 WUSD Policy Reminder: Using the `MI` flag allows front-office clerks to pull instant missing assignment lists for academic intervention sweeps.
Affected Tables: GRD, SSS

Avoid copying details by hand. If you teach three identical periods of the same grade level (e.g., Period 1, 3, and 5 Science), use the copy wizard to duplicate columns instantly.

How to Do This:
  1. Open the gradebook containing your master assignment. Click the **Assignments** tab.
  2. Locate the target assignment card. Click **Edit** (or select the copy icon).
  3. A list of adjacent linked sections taught by you will display at the bottom.
  4. Check the boxes next to your other class periods (e.g., Period 3, Period 5).
  5. Click **Save & Copy**. The assignment structure will write to all selected gradebooks.
⚠️ Common Mistake: Modifying the assignment name in one period *after* copying. This breaks mapping parameters if you are syncing from Canvas, generating duplicate columns! Keep names consistent.
Affected Tables: MST, SMS

By default, newly created or Canvas-imported assignments are sometimes saved in "Draft" format, hiding them on parent dashboards to prevent parent emails before all rosters are graded.

How to Do This:
  1. Open the active gradebook, select **Assignments**, and click on the target column header.
  2. Click **Edit Assignment**.
  3. Locate the checkbox labeled "Publish to Portal" (or "Show to Parent/Student").
  4. Check the box and click **Save**. Grades will instantly display on family dashboards.
Affected Tables: SSS, CON

Taking roll call is a legal and financial mandate. If your class is in session, submit attendance as normal inside Aeries. If the period is locked, you must contact support.

How to Do This:
  1. If your school's daily period window has passed, the attendance cell will show a lock icon, preventing further teacher inputs.
  2. Do not attempt to resubmit multiple empty rosters.
  3. Email your site's **Attendance Clerk** directly. Provide the Student Name, ID Number, Period, and exact status code (e.g., Present, Tardy with arrival time, or Excused Illness).
  4. The clerk will manually override the locked ledger using the administration console.
⚠️ Legal Compulsory Warning: Under California Ed Code, teachers have exactly 3 days to adjust rolls before attendance calendars freeze permanently for state ADA audits.
Affected Tables: ATT

If you have an inclusion specialist, RSP teacher, or a student teacher supporting your sections, you can grant them access to your gradebook without sharing your account password.

How to Do This:
  1. Open the target Gradebook and click Manage Gradebook.
  2. Click the **Users** or **Associate Teachers** tab.
  3. Click **Add User**. Type the co-teacher's official district email address.
  4. Assign their permission level:
    • Read-Only: Allows viewing of score calculations and reports, but prevents editing or adding assignments.
    • Co-Owner: Full edit privileges (ideal for student teachers or shared rosters).
  5. Click **Save**. The gradebook card will display on their dashboard instantly upon login.
Affected Tables: STF, SMS

When the front office registers a student, Aeries automatically links them during the 3:00 AM nightly database sync. You can bypass this wait and pull the student immediately.

How to Do This:
  1. Open your active Gradebook and click Manage Students.
  2. Click the button labeled "Add Students".
  3. Aeries will scan active demographics for newly registered schedules.
  4. Check the box next to the student's name and click **Add to Gradebook**. They will instantly occupy a row in your spreadsheet.
Affected Tables: STU, SEC
