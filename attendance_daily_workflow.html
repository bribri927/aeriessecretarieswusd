📝 3. Attendance & Daily Workflow • Legal Compliance

Attendance is More Than Roll Call

Taking attendance in California is a legal mandate, a primary funding mechanism (Average Daily Attendance - ADA), and a critical safety directive. In this workspace, you will learn the exact steps to record daily roll-call, correct minor entries within legal timelines, arrange physical seating chart maps in Aeries, and prepare stress-free substitutes rosters. Keep this guide tiled next to your active database workspace window!

What happens if attendance isn't submitted?

Under California Education Code, a school's funding is calculated directly through student daily physical presence. Every single classroom attendance record that remains "Unsubmitted" by the designated daily period window triggers immediate operational issues:

  • Funding Loss: The school district is legally prohibited from claiming state average daily attendance (ADA) revenue for unverified classrooms.
  • Emergency Liability: In the event of an active evacuation, drill, or campus lock-down, the front office has no official digital record of where students are physically located.
  • Office Interruption: Unsubmitted rosters force front office staff to call your classroom directly, interrupting instructional time during critical periods.

Daily Operation • Step-by-Step

How to Submit Daily Attendance

Whether you teach a self-contained elementary classroom or a rotating secondary block, the daily submission workflow inside your active Aeries database follows this exact chronological structure:

Left Navigation → Attendance → Take Attendance (or click the Green Check box on your Dashboard)

Standard Submission Workflow:

  1. Open the attendance module. By default, Aeries loads your classroom alphabetical roster with empty cells representing **Present**.
  2. Look over your classroom desks. Leave physical present students completely **Blank** in the cell. Do not key codes for present students.
  3. Identify physically absent students. Click the cell adjacent to their name and select **Absent (A)** from the status dropdown.
  4. If a student arrives late within your site's tardy threshold window, click their cell to switch the status to **Tardy (T)**.
  5. Review class count summaries at the top. Click **Submit Attendance**.
  6. The Receipt Badge: Ensure a solid green checkmark or confirmation banner renders in your top status header. If it stays orange or blank, your roll has not been saved inside database files!
⚠️ Common Roster Mistake:

Marking tardy students as absent. If a student walks in late, you must update their status cell to Tardy (T). Leaving them marked Absent (A) triggers automated truancy emails and voice calls to their parent, resulting in avoidable front-desk panic.

Operational Errors • Step-by-Step

Correcting Attendance Errors

Did you accidentally mark the wrong student absent? Or did a student walk in late after you submitted your daily roll? Teachers can easily execute minor corrections within a 3-day compliance window.

Open Attendance sheet → Click 'Change' or target Period row → Update code

How to Correct an Entry:

  1. Re-open your active class period attendance card in Aeries.
  2. Locate the row of the student requiring adjustments.
  3. Click their active status cell (e.g., change `A` back to `Blank` for present, or click `A` to toggle to `T` for tardy).
  4. Click the **Submit Attendance** button again to seal the updated ledger.
  5. Locked Records: If the 3-day site correction window has passed, the attendance cell will show a lock icon. You cannot edit locked data. Email your site attendance clerk immediately with the student's name, ID, and correction details.

Classroom Architecture • Layout Controls

Setting Up Seating Charts in Aeries

Aeries features a native drag-and-drop Seating Chart builder. Using a seating chart simplifies attendance and enables substitutes to identify students.

Attendance sheet → Click 'Seating Chart' button at Top Right

Step-by-Step Chart Design:

  1. Open the Seating Chart tool. You will see an empty canvas on the left and your roster list of students on the right.
  2. Use the grid layout tools to define your room's rows and columns (e.g., a 5x6 desk array).
  3. Click and drag each student card from your roster panel directly onto its designated desk block on the canvas.
  4. Toggle the option **"Show Student Photos"** to display school ID pictures on the seating chart.
  5. Click **Save Layout**. When taking attendance daily, you can toggle the 'Chart View' to simply click on desks to mark absences.

Specialized Workflows • Compliance

Short-Term Independent Study & Substitute Rosters

Teachers must coordinate specialized actions when students are on approved Independent Study contracts, or when a substitute instructor is managing the daily roll.

Independent Study (I Code)

If a student has a pre-approved Short-Term Independent Study contract:

  • The office will flag their roster row with a specialized pending marker.
  • Coordinate and deliver academic assignments before they depart.
  • Upon return, grade and evaluate their completed work to determine academic credit hours, enabling the clerk to clear their attendance code.
Preparing for a Substitute

Substitutes do not have direct digital access to write to your live Aeries database:

  • Open your Aeries attendance portal and select **Print Attendance Slip**.
  • Leave this physical, printed roster checklist alongside your physical lesson plans.
  • The substitute will mark absences manually and deliver the signed slip to the front office for database entry.
