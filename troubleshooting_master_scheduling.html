Stuck? Let's Diagnose the Issues Together

We've all been there: you run a master schedule simulation, and your placement rate is stuck at 40%, classrooms are showing double-booked, or teachers have more sections than contracts allow. This page serves as a live triage manual. Open your active Aeries database on one screen and keep this workspace open on your second screen as a step-by-step diagnostic companion.

The Core Diagnostic Strategy

Master scheduling failures are almost always categorized into two distinct types of errors. Understanding which side of the scale you are on saves hours of blind re-building:

1. Student Request Failures (The SSS Table)

Students have bad course requests, duplicates, or missing core prerequisites. The scheduling engine runs out of choices because of the student's constraints, leaving them unscheduled.

2. Master Board Failures (The MST/SMS Tables)

Teachers are double-booked, class maximum capacities are capped too low, singletons are clashing during the same period, or class calendars are unlinked.

Live Verification Diagnostics

  • Class Overloads: Spotting enrollment numbers that breach contracted limits.
  • Double-Booked Rooms: Preventing physical room overrides.
  • Unscheduled Students: Islating students left with gaps or empty periods.
  • Missing Calendar Flags: Fixing date ranges on sections in the master.

The 4 Essential Troubleshooting Scenarios

When you encounter a scheduling dead-end, work through these four critical scenarios to resolve your bottlenecks.

Scenario 1 — Your Scheduling Placement Rate is Low (Under 80%)

Diagnostic Scenario 1 • Scheduling Passes

Goal: Diagnose why the student scheduling simulator is unable to place students into valid paths, resulting in widespread gaps in student rosters.

Aeries Path:

Scheduling Process → Reports → Conflict Matrix / Student Schedule Summary

Triage Protocol (Step-by-Step):

  1. Open your **Conflict Matrix** report. This creates a grid intersecting every singleton and doubleton course at your school.
  2. Look for high intersection numbers. If AP Literature (Period 1) and AP Government (Period 1) have an intersection of 25, it means 25 students requested both courses, but they are physically locked in the same period block.
  3. Spread these sections across the master schedule (e.g., move AP Literature to Period 3).
  4. Re-run the scheduling pass. Your placement rate should immediately rise.
The 80% Benchmark: If your placement rate starts below 80% on the first run, do not attempt to schedule individual students manually. You have a structural conflict on the master board (typically overlapping singletons). Resolve the pattern conflict first to save hours of manual counseling schedules.

Scenario 2 — Class Overloads & Max Capacity Collisions

Diagnostic Scenario 2 • Seat Balance Audit

Goal: Detect and resolve sections where enrollment counts breach district-contracted limits, leaving classes imbalanced.

Aeries Path:

Scheduling Process → Master Schedule → Interactive Master Schedule

Triage Protocol (Step-by-Step):

  1. Run a query or view the Interactive Master Schedule Board to review section counts side-by-side.
  2. Find the maximum class limit setting on the master schedule record (MST.MX). If a classroom is contracted for a maximum of 32 students, ensure `MST.MX` is set to exactly 32.
  3. If a course is showing overfilled (e.g., 38 students in standard English 10, while another section has 12), use the Aeries **Interactive Scheduling Board** to drag and drop students to balance class sizes.
Beginner Mistake: Artificially raising the maximum seat limit `MST.MX` in Aeries to clear scheduling blocks without consulting site administration. This causes room overcrowding and contract limit violations.

Scenario 3 — Teacher and Room Overlaps

Diagnostic Scenario 3 • Room & Instructor Collision Audit

Goal: Find and repair physical room overrides and teacher double-bookings (where an instructor is scheduled to teach two classes in the same period).

Aeries Path:

Scheduling Process → Reports → Room Overlap Listing / Teacher Load Report

Triage Protocol (Step-by-Step):

  1. Run the **Room Overlap Listing** to identify instances where multiple sections are assigned to the same room during the same period. This frequently occurs with shared spaces like gyms, science labs, or multi-purpose rooms.
  2. Run the **Teacher Load Report** to confirm no teacher is assigned to multiple classes in the same period.
  3. Correct overlaps by assigning unique classroom numbers or updating the section teacher assignment records on the Master Board.

Scenario 4 — Senior Graduation Path Holds

Diagnostic Scenario 4 • 12th Grade Triage

Goal: Prioritize graduation-critical scheduling runs for seniors and credit-deficient students, ensuring they receive required courses before general electives are scheduled.

Aeries Path:

Scheduling Process → Student Data → Graduation Requirements (REQ) / Transcripts (HIS)

Triage Protocol (Step-by-Step):

  1. Isolate senior records. Group student requests by graduation track.
  2. Lock the required core courses for seniors first. This protects their graduation pathways and ensures they are placed before lower grade levels fill elective seats.
  3. Run scheduling passes with priority flags enabled for the senior cohort.

Aeries Master Database Field Reference Sheet

When running custom audits or writing advanced Aeries queries, refer to this cheat sheet of key database tables and fields to ensure your diagnostics are targeting the right parameters:

Table Field Name Database Code Common Troubleshooting Use Cases
MST Max Capacity MST.MX Audit seat capacities and resolve class-overload limits.
MST Actual Enrollment MST.ACT Audit class sizes and balance student rosters across periods.
SSS Course Number SSS.CN Clean up student requests and remove duplicate or invalid course entries.
SMS Class Calendar Index SMS.CSQ Link flex periods to valid calendars and prevent attendance audit issues.
SMS Exclusion Flag SMS.ES Verify exclusion flags on assembly days or modified schedules.
