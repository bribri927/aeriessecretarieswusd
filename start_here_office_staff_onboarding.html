Aeries Basics: Teach Me From Scratch

Welcome to WUSD! If you have never opened a student information database before, you are in the right place. Open this guide in one browser window and open Aeries in another window next to it. We will build your knowledge step-by-step.

🖥️

The "Split Screen" Practice Method

Do not just read this! For the best learning experience, snap this window to the **left side** of your monitor, and open Aeries on the **right side**. Every step is written as an active action you can immediately perform and test.

Your Onboarding Roadmap

Complete these milestones sequentially on your first day of system access.

1
Milestone 1 • The Security Check

Logging In & Setting Your Site

Before touching student records, you must confirm you are logged into the correct database. It is incredibly common to accidentally search the wrong year or have your settings locked to the district office.

What to Do in Your Aeries Window:
  1. Log in using your secure WUSD single sign-on credentials (SSO).
  2. Look in the **top-right corner** of your active header. You will see a dropdown indicating your active school year and active school site.
  3. Verify your year: Ensure it says the current academic year (e.g., 2026-2027) and not last year.
  4. Verify your school: If you work at an elementary school but the site is accidentally set to 'District Office', you will not find your site's students or menus!
Aeries Header (Top Right) → Site Selector Dropdown → Verify Site Match
🚨 Why this matters:

If you enter demographic corrections or attendance codes while logged into the wrong site, you will trigger severe reporting errors across the entire district database cluster.

2
Milestone 2 • Interface Navigation

Decoding the Aeries Layout Map

The Aeries interface is divided into three primary zones. Familiarizing yourself with these zones means you won't get lost when following complex procedural instructions.

Zone A: Left Menu Bar

Your primary navigation panel. Folders slide open to reveal screens like **Student Data**, **Attendance**, and **Medical**.

Zone B: Header Search

The main search input. Typing here allows you to quickly query names, student IDs, or grade cohorts.

Zone C: Active Form Card

The main middle area where student records, contact cards, and daily calendars display for edits.

Navigation Skill Check:
  1. Move to the left menu. Hover over **Student Data**. Click it to expand.
  2. Find **Demographics** in the expanded list. Click it once.
  3. Confirm the middle area changes to display the main student information layout. You have successfully navigated your first path!
Left Sidebar → Student Data → Demographics
3
Milestone 3 • Query Skills

Mastering Student Search Operators

Searching by typing out entire first and last names is slow and prone to spelling errors. Aeries supports unique **Search Operator** symbols that allow you to locate records instantly with brief syntax commands.

🔮 Interactive Search Syntax Practice Simulator

Select or type a query search syntax below to see exactly how Aeries filters database records.

Active Search Query: Smi, Jo
Database Action:

Looks for students whose last names begin with "Smi" and first names begin with "Jo" (e.g., Joseph Smith, Joanna Smithers).

4
Milestone 4 • Database Structure

The Core 4 Database Tables Defined

Every piece of student data you interact with is filed in database categories called "Tables." Think of student profiles as drawers in a filing cabinet. Each drawer has several specialized cards inside:

STU Table
Demographics Card

The master anchor file. Holds Student ID, legal name, date of birth, grade level, and enrollment status (e.g., Active or Inactive).

CON Table
Contacts Card

Stores parents, guardians, emergency release contacts, priority phone numbers, and web portal access associations.

ATT Table
Attendance History Card

Records every single absence mark, tardy timestamp, checkout code, and parent-excusal note for all 180 instructional days.

SEC Table
Class Schedule Card

Links the student to specific courses, period blocks, rooms, teachers, and corresponding grading gradebooks.

5
Milestone 5 • Daily Workflow Basics

Your First Roster Lookup Task

Let's put your navigation and search operator knowledge to work. Imagine a parent walks into your school lobby and asks to verify who their child's third-period teacher is.

Follow this Build-Along Checklist:
  1. In your Aeries Search bar, type the student's **ID number** (if known) or their last name (using the operator format `Smi, Jo`).
  2. Once the demographics screen opens, navigate to the sidebar menu and select **Classes** or **Schedule**.
  3. Locate the period column on the screen and look up the row representing **Period 3**.
  4. Identify the teacher's name and room number assigned to that period row. You can now confidently assist the family!
Aeries Header Search → Student Profile → Left Menu: Student Data → Classes

⚠️ Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

Be on alert for these four common pitfalls on your first week.

1. Not checking global duplicates

When a family enrolls a child, always search the entire district history first before creating a brand-new profile. Re-creating an existing student splits academic transcripts.

2. Modifying Contacts from secondary cards

Always check the primary emergency contact card links. Modifying details on secondary student cards without verification can disconnect parent-portal access accounts.

3. Forgetting site context checks

Entering attendance changes or scheduling blocks while your site dropdown remains set to 'District Office' blocks synchronization updates.

4. Back-dating logs

Never log back-dated attendance changes without checking independent study rosters. Back-dating blindly can delete state-audited instruction records.
