Principal Guides

🧭 Interactive Scheduling Walkthrough

Start Here
1. Prepare inputs.
Verify enrollment projections, staffing, bell schedule, rooms, course offerings, and program needs before opening Aeries.
2. Collect and verify requests.
Confirm student requests and alternates. For secondary schools, check required courses before electives.
3. Build sections.
Create sections with teacher, room, period, term, seat count, and scheduling attributes. Schedule singletons early.
4. Run scheduling.
Mass schedule students, then review unscheduled students, conflicts, teacher loads, and rooms.
5. Adjust and re-run.
Fix patterns, not just individual students. Add/move sections, adjust singletons, and re-run.
6. Validate and submit.
Principal/counselor review, checklist completion, documented exceptions, and local approval process.

🚨 Scheduling Troubleshooting

Fast Fixes
Too many students unscheduled
Check section availability, singleton conflicts, missing alternates, required courses offered in too few periods, and teacher/room restrictions.
Flex schedule confusion
Confirm the flex purpose, attendance process, assignment process, supervision plan, and service conflicts.
Teacher overloads
Run teacher schedule/load reports. Check duplicate sections, wrong terms, and incorrect assignments.
Electives disappeared
Review course priorities, alternate requests, and whether required singletons are blocking electives.
Submission readiness: enrollment verified, course requests complete, sections built, teachers/rooms assigned, conflicts reviewed, unscheduled students documented, counselor/admin review complete.
