Playbook 1 • California Vaccine Compliance

Immunization Auditing & California Mandates

Under California Health and Safety Code Sections 120325-120375, schools are prohibited from allowing students on campus unless their immunization record is complete, conditionally met, or backed by a legally registered medical exemption.

Target Auditing Cohorts & Specific Doses Required:

Grade TK / Kindergarten Entry Requirements:
  • Polio (IPV): 4 doses (3 doses are acceptable if the last dose was given on or after the 4th birthday).
  • DTaP: 5 doses (4 doses acceptable if the last dose was on or after the 4th birthday).
  • MMR (Measles, Mumps, Rubella): 2 doses (both given on or after the 1st birthday).
  • Hepatitis B: 3 doses.
  • Varicella (Chickenpox): 2 doses.
Grade 7 Entry Booster Requirements:
  • Tdap (Tetanus, Diphtheria, Pertussis): 1 dose given on or after the 7th birthday.
  • Varicella (Chickenpox): 2 doses (typically verified during original TK/K entry, but must be checked for new out-of-state enrollments).

Entering Vaccine Records into Aeries:

  1. Search for the student ID. Navigate to the Immunization module.
    Aeries Navigation: Student Data → Medical → Immunizations (IMM)
  2. Click **Change** at the bottom of the immunization form.
  3. Enter the exact date (MM/DD/YY) for each dose. Do not enter checkmarks or place placeholders; enter complete dates from the official yellow card or CAIR (California Immunization Registry) system.
  4. If the student is entering with a registered CAIR Medical Exemption, enter the CAIR Exemption Number directly into the specialized exemption fields and save.
  5. Double-check the **Immunization Status** dropdown (e.g., set to "Has Requirements" or "Conditional"). Click **Save** to lock.
🚨 Critical Audit Penalty Notice:

State auditors pull sample student records annually. Any active student on campus without a completed immunization record, conditional admission timeline, or validated CAIR exemption represents a legal violation, exposing the school district to immediate funding audits.

Playbook 2 • Emergency Management

Critical Health Alerts & Medical Icons

Students with severe, life-threatening medical conditions (such as anaphylactic food allergies, diabetes, or epilepsy) must have active **Health Alerts** flagged. Flagging the alert generates a bright red medical cross next to the student's name on all teacher portals and office search screens, notifying teachers immediately.

How to Flag a Critical Health Alert:

  1. Search for the student to load their Demographic profile.
  2. Locate and open the Medical History module.
    Aeries Navigation: Student Data → Medical → Medical History (MED)
  3. Click the **Add New Record** button.
  4. Select the correct medical code (e.g., **Allergy - Peanut (AL1)**, **Asthma (AS)**, or **Diabetes Type 1 (DB1)**) from the dropdown list.
  5. The Crucial Flag: Check the box labeled **Flag as a Health Alert** (or "Alert Flag"). Setting this is what renders the red cross icon on teacher portals.
  6. In the **Details/Comment** text box, write clear, brief instructions for teachers.
    Example Text: "Peanut allergy. Requires EpiPen in health office. Call 911 immediately if exposed."
  7. Set the **Status** to active, and click **Save**. Always upload the Emergency Action Plan to the Documents tab.

Playbook 3 • Daily Operations

Clinic Log Entries & Daily Medications

Every visit to the health office must be documented chronologically in the Medical Log (`ML` table). Daily or emergency medications kept on campus must be documented alongside signed physician orders.

Documenting Nurse Clinic Visits (ML Table)

When a student comes to the health office with an injury, fever, or illness, log the encounter immediately:

  1. Navigate to the Medical Log screen.
    Aeries Navigation: Student Data → Medical → Medical Log (ML)
  2. Click **Add New Log Entry**.
  3. Select the **Visit Type** (e.g., Sick, Injury, Medication Admin).
  4. Enter the **Time In** and **Disposition** (e.g., Returned to Class, Sent Home, Parent Pick-up).
  5. In the **Comments** area, note findings objectively:
    Objective Entry Example: "Complaining of stomachache. Temp 98.6°F. Rested for 15 mins. Returned to class."
  6. Click **Save** to create the official legal timeline of the encounter.

Logging Daily Medication Administration

Students receiving scheduled prescription medication (e.g., ADHD medication at lunch) must have their doses logged upon administration:

  1. Verify that a signed **Physician Authorization Form** is kept in the health folder.
  2. Under the Medical History page, ensure the prescription, dosage, and scheduled time are configured.
  3. When the student receives their dose, open the **Medication Log** or Daily Medical Log.
  4. Log the date, time of administration, and the clerk or nurse's signature. If the student fails to arrive, record the encounter as a "No-Show" and call the classroom teacher.

Playbook 4 • Clinical Referrals

Vision, Hearing & Mandated Screenings

Mandated Screening Cohorts (California Ed Code)

California mandates regular health screenings. Health clerks coordinate, log, and follow up on referrals:

  • Vision Screenings: Required in Kindergarten, Grade 2, Grade 5, Grade 8, and upon first school entry.
  • Hearing Screenings: Required in Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 5, Grade 8, Grade 10, and upon first entry.

How to Log Screenings & Referral Status in Aeries:

  1. Open the student's profile. Select Hearing or Vision screen under the Medical menu.
    Aeries Navigation: Student Data → Medical → Hearing / Vision
  2. Click **Add New Screening**. Enter the screening date.
  3. Input raw measurements (e.g., Right Eye 20/20, Left Eye 20/40) or Pass/Fail results.
  4. Processing Referrals: If the student fails their initial screening and rescreening, set the **Referral Status** to "Referred".
  5. Generate a parent referral packet containing localized clinic resources and mail it home.
  6. Upon receiving the returned, signed medical professional report, update the referral record in Aeries with the disposition (e.g., "Glasses Prescribed").
