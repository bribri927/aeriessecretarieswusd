🚀 1. Start Here • Class Onboarding

New to Aeries? Or Just Need a Refresher?

Don’t worry—you can do this, and we’ll walk you through it step-by-step. This onboarding guide is structured to sit open on one half of your screen while you log in and prepare your active classroom rosters, categories, and grading periods inside your real database window. Let's make sure everything runs smoothly before your students arrive!

1
Milestone 1 • Navigation

Log In & Learn the Layout Map

The secret to mastering Aeries is knowing where key classroom functions are located. Let’s log in and look around your main menu bar so you can comfortably move between your rosters and active gradebooks.

What this helps you do:

Locate essential teaching functions (like rosters, daily attendance grids, and scorebooks) without searching through general system options.

What to click:
  • Open your district Aeries portal page and select Teacher Portal.
  • Type in your secure WUSD single sign-on (SSO) email.
  • Find the left-hand navigation sidebar on your home screen.
  • Click on Attendance to view your daily roll, or expand Gradebook to check active assignments.
What to look for:
  • Roster View: Look for your students’ demographic summaries.
  • Gradebook Card: Your digital grade calculation sheets.
  • Messaging Panel: Automate missing work emails directly to parents.
  • Active Year: Make sure the top right corner shows the current academic year.
🚨 Common Onboarding Mistake:

Trying to learn everything at once. Aeries is a robust database, but classroom teachers only need to interact with a tiny portion of it. Focus exclusively on setting up your gradebooks, recording daily attendance, and updating assignments during your first weeks!

2
Milestone 2 • Setup Sequence

Set Up Your Digital Gradebooks

Let’s initialize your digital gradebooks. Setting up your rules early ensures your scores calculate exactly how you want throughout the semester.

Left Sidebar → Gradebook → Click [Add Gradebook] button
Your Step-by-Step Gradebook Setup:
  1. Select Course Sections: Check the boxes next to matching classes to link sections of the same subject.
  2. Choose Term Grading Periods: Match your active grading term parameters (e.g., Semester 1 or Trimester 1).
  3. Add Categories: Establish clean category buckets (e.g., Homework, Tests, Projects).
  4. Apply Weighting Rules: If your syllabus uses weighted values, check "Weight Categories" and enter percentage proportions. Weighted grades are calculated using the standard formula:
    $\text{Final Grade} = \sum (\text{Category Score} \times \text{Category Weight})$
  5. Set Assignment Defaults: Configure default max points (e.g., 100) and decide whether late work automatically receives a missing marker.
  6. Safe-Copy Configurations: If you taught this course previously, use the **Copy Settings** utility to duplicate configurations and rubrics from previous terms to save time.
❓ What happens if I skip this?

If you don't define categories and weights first, Aeries will treat all entered assignments with equal points weight. A 10-point quick quiz will carry the exact same mathematical weight as a 10-point final exam, which can lead to unexpected grading errors later!

3
Milestone 3 • Daily Compliance

Take Your Daily Attendance

Daily attendance is a key legal and funding mandate. Your rolls must be submitted during your designated period window.

Left Sidebar → Attendance (or click the green 'Take Attendance' box on your home dashboard)
Your Attendance Workflow:
  1. Open the attendance grid. It will list your students alphabetically alongside a daily grid.
  2. Leave students who are physically in their seats as **Present (Blank)**. By default, Aeries assumes everyone is present.
  3. Click the status cell for absent students to mark them **Absent (A)**.
  4. If a student arrives late, update their status to **Tardy (T)** and record their arrival time if required.
  5. Short-Term Independent Study (IS): If a student has an active, pre-approved IS contract, leave them marked as **Present/Independent Study (I)** based on counselor instructions.
  6. Click **Submit Attendance** to lock your roster. A green confirmation badge will render in your header.
💡 How to correct mistakes:

If you make an attendance error (e.g., marking a late student absent), you can adjust codes and resubmit within your school's open submission window. If the window has closed, email your site attendance clerk immediately with the correction details.

4
Milestone 4 • Gradebook Management

Add an Assignment

Once your gradebook structure is initialized, you can begin adding assignments as students complete coursework.

Open Gradebook → Click 'Assignments' tab → Click 'Add Assignment'
Key Parameters to Populate:
  • Name & Description: Write clear titles (e.g., *Ch. 1 Vocabulary homework*) so parents understand the assignment.
  • Assign & Due Dates: Record accurate dates. This determines which grading period is credited.
  • Category: Select the correct linked category bucket (e.g., Tests, Assignments).
  • Scoring Rules: Set the maximum points value (e.g., 50) and grading scale.
  • Publish Visibility: Toggle the **Publish to Parent/Student Portal** flag. If unchecked, the assignment is hidden on family dashboards.
5
Milestone 5 • Scorekeeping

Enter Grades & Scores

Aeries offers several ways to input grades. Choose the method that best fits your classroom setup.

Option A: Quick Grid Input

Open your full gradebook spreadsheet card, double-click individual cells, and type scores directly. Press **Enter** to save and move down.

Option B: Mass Grade Input

Select a specific assignment, and click **Mass Entry**. This allows you to apply a baseline grade (e.g., 10/10) to the entire class, and adjust individual scores for missing work.

WUSD Standard Grade Flags & Tags:
  • MI (Missing): Counts as a zero, flagging the student profile and triggering parent missing work notifications.
  • EX (Excused): Bypasses grade calculations, preventing the assignment from impacting the student's average.
  • Comments: Click the comment bubble on a student's row to add notes (e.g., *"Late, turned in 2 days over"*).
6
Milestone 6 • Family Transparency

Parent & Student Portal Visibility

Aeries is a transparent system. When you input grades, they can immediately impact student and parent dashboards.

Keep these district visibility guidelines in mind:

  • Immediate Updates: Once a score is entered, it is displayed on family portal dashboards unless the assignment is explicitly set to "Hidden/Unpublished."
  • Grading Drafts: If you are working on a multi-week essay or project, uncheck **Publish to Portal** until you have graded all rosters. This prevents parent emails before grades are finalized.
  • LMS Sync Expectations: If you sync grades from Canvas or Google Classroom, remember sync intervals run overnight. Allow up to 24 hours for grades to update in Aeries before manual adjustments.
7
Milestone 7 • Classroom Triage

Troubleshoot Common Problems

If you encounter an issue, don't worry—most problems can be resolved quickly. Here is how to fix the most common teacher roadblocks:

Q: "My gradebook scores have disappeared!"

Fix: You are likely logged into last year's database or a different school term. Check the site year dropdown in the top-right corner, and verify that your gradebook active term is configured correctly.

Q: "My Canvas/Google Classroom assignments are not syncing to Aeries."

Fix: Your categories must match exactly. If Canvas has a group named "Tests" (plural) but Aeries has "Test" (singular), the sync will fail. Check and align your category names.

Q: "Attendance won't save, and I see a warning banner."

Fix: This happens if you are attempting to take attendance outside of the district's open scheduling period. Check that your class period matches your active session.
