Playbook 1 • Enrollment Processing

How to Enroll a Student Step-by-Step

Student registration is the gateway to CALPADS state reports. When a family requests enrollment, follow this sequence in your active Aeries window:

Aeries Click Path & Live Execution Steps:

  1. Perform a global duplicate search: Before adding a new student, check if they exist in historical records anywhere in the district database.
    Left Sidebar → Student Data → Search All Students

    Search by partial first/last name and date of birth. If you find them, request a records transfer rather than creating a duplicate profile.

  2. Collect and verify residency documentation: Confirm they live within boundaries using a utility bill, lease, or mortgage document. (See document check in sidebar).
  3. Add the Demographics Card (STU): If completely new, select "Add" to generate a blank demographics layout.
    Left Sidebar → Student Data → Demographics → Click [Add]

    Input legal name exactly as it matches their birth certificate. Enter enrollment date, status code 'Active' (A), and district boundary code.

  4. Document Uploading: Once demographics are saved, immediately scan and load support materials directly to the record.
    Student Card (Demographics) → Select 'Documents' Tab → Click [Add Document]

    Upload files with logical names (e.g., "Immunization_Record.pdf", "Proof_Of_Residency.pdf"). Avoid generic file names.

🚨 Critical Boundary Reminder:

Never enroll an out-of-district student without an approved Inter-District Transfer Permit logged inside the Specialized Programs module. Unapproved boundary entries generate compliance warnings during state audits.

Playbook 2 • Exit & Transfer Sequences

How to Process a Student Withdrawal

Exiting a student cleanly is vital. Leaving students active when they are no longer physically attending can trigger truancy counts and mess up the school's ADA metrics.

Exit Sequence in Aeries:

  1. Audit active attendance parameters: Look up the student in Aeries and verify their last day of physical attendance. You **cannot** withdraw a student on a day they were marked present.
    Left Sidebar → Attendance → Attendance Detail → Verify Last Present Day
  2. Trigger the Exit update: Open the Demographics card and click **Change**.
    Student Data → Demographics → Click [Change]
  3. Set Exit codes: Modify **Status** from 'Active' (A) to 'Inactive' (or the custom exit code used at your site). Input the **Exit Date** (first day of non-attendance) and the correct **Exit Reason Code** (e.g., Transfer to another CA public school).
  4. Drop Scheduled Sections: Clicking save automatically drops all future course scheduling sections.
  5. Organize Cumulative Records (Cum Files): Gather the physical folder, print an official **Aeries Transcript / Course History (HIS)**, and write down the date and destination school name on the folder tab before archiving.

Playbook 3 • Address Changes & Portal Management

Managing Address and Contact Changes

Address and emergency contact safety checks prevent unauthorized individuals from picking up students and keep mailing communications going to the right place.

How to Update Address or Phone:

Address updates require proof of residency documentation before editing demographics.

  1. Verify the residency document (utility bill, lease, etc.) matches the parent's legal name.
  2. Open Demographics (STU) in Aeries, click **Change**, and type in the corrected address fields.
  3. Save and upload the proof of residency document directly to the Documents tab.
Student Data → Demographics → Click [Change] → Address/Phone fields

Managing Emergency Contacts (CON table):

Always check the contact lists and relationship fields before editing records.

  1. Navigate to the **Contacts (CON)** menu on the sidebar.
  2. Verify who has legal custody of the student. Check the legal documentation inside cumulative records.
  3. Ensure the **Contact Order / Priority** (e.g., Priority 1, 2, 3) is updated and parent permissions (e.g., Educational Rights, Mailings, Portal Access) are correctly flagged.
Left Sidebar → Student Data → Contacts (CON) → Click [Update/Add]

Troubleshooting Parent Portal Access:

If a parent cannot connect to their child's portal dashboard, check their credentials in your Aeries window.

  1. Confirm the parent's email address is saved in the Contacts (CON) record.
  2. Check the portal verification code under the **Portal Accounts** utility.
  3. Provide the parent with the **Student ID**, **Primary Phone Number**, and **Verification Code** so they can easily link the profile.
Student Data → Medical / Demographics → Portal Accounts (Audit verification code)

Playbook 4 • Secretary Attendance Auditing

Attendance Correction and Daily Reports

As a secretary, you are often the first line of defense for morning attendance questions. Make sure corrections follow this workflow:

The Standard Correction Path:
  1. Confirm who called or sent the excuse note.
  2. Search for the student, and open the **Attendance Detail** page.
  3. Click **Update**, select the target date, and use standard codes: E (Excused Illness) or A (Unexcused).
  4. Always enter call comments explaining who requested the excuse before saving.
Attendance → Attendance Detail → Click [Update]
Essential Reports to Print:
  • Student Directory / Locator Cards: Prints room schedules for office staff front-desk lookups.
  • Missing Attendance Report: Run daily to find teachers who haven't completed attendance calls.
  • Emergency Contact Sheets: Prints physical lists for fire drills or school emergency situations.
Aeries Header → Select 'Reports' → Type Report Name

📋 Required Document Check

Always verify these three baseline documents before saving an active demographic (STU) record:

1. Proof of Age (Birth Certificate)

Matches legal name exactly as entered in Aeries. Check spelling and middle names.

2. Verification of Residency

Current SMUD bill, PGE bill, lease agreement, or property tax statement showing the parent's name.

3. Complete Immunization Cards

Required shots completed. (Routinely audited by the site health clerk before the first day of instruction).

📅 EOY Rollover Reminders

As you prepare for school year transitions, make sure your data checks are completed:

1. Close Out Independent Study contracts

Ensure all active attendance lines for short-term independent studies are compiled and archived before system freeze dates.

2. Cumulative Record Audits

Check that printed transcript slips match final gradebook data in student files before folders rollover to the next grade.
