✅ Submission Audit Checklist

The Final Master Schedule Pre-Flight Check

Before submitting your draft schedule to your district committee or unlocking it for public viewing, you must perform a comprehensive data validation pass. Keep this workbook open on one side of your monitor as a guide, and run each live verification step inside your Aeries database window side-by-side.

Live Pre-Flight Audit Status

0 of 15 verification steps completed (0%)

1

Enrollment Sizing & Core Allocation Checks

Match Section Capacity Against Enrollment Limits

Open your Master Board and verify that the Class Section Limits (MST.MX) match contracted student caps exactly. Overlooking this step creates localized class sizing imbalances during student scheduling runs.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Master Schedule → Interactive Schedule Board
Where to look: Check that general education courses are set to standard limits (e.g., 30) and that specialized Special Ed blocks are capped appropriately.

Audit Sections with Zero Active Enrollment

Scan your current Master Schedule draft to identify sections where the active headcount is 0. Verify whether these represent unplaced courses that should be deleted, or holding sections designated for late registrants.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Reports → Master Schedule Board List
Auditing Query: LIST MST CRS MST.SE MST.CN CRS.CO MST.ACT IF MST.ACT = 0

Review Grade-Level Course Request Alignments

Confirm that your total core section counts (English, Math, History, Science) meet grade-level projections. If incoming counts require 6 sections, but you only created 5, students will be locked out of required subjects.

Aeries Staging: Reports → Enrollment Reports → Course Request Tallies
2

Master Board Structural & Calendar Sanity Checks

Verify Flex Class Calendar Index Links

If your campus runs under a Flex period layout, every section must be explicitly linked to an active Class Calendar sequence (SMS.CSQ). Unlinked flex periods generate attendance auditing failures and gradebook errors.

Aeries Staging: Attendance Accounting → Flex Period Setup → Class Calendars
Diagnostic Check: Ensure that all standard academic courses are assigned with calendar index 1, and alternate rotations are assigned with respective A/B calendars.

Check for Double-Booked Classrooms (Room overlaps)

Scan your schedule to ensure no physical room is scheduled for multiple sections during the same instructional period. This check is crucial for shared rooms, including gyms, computer labs, and science classrooms.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Reports → Room Overlap Listing

Validate Term Layout Definitions (Year vs. Semester vs. Quarters)

Ensure that matching multi-part courses (e.g., Semester 1 and Semester 2 course pairs) are assigned to the correct term fields on the section card. Out-of-alignment codes prevent gradebooks from carrying over credits.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Master Schedule → Sections Detail
3

Staffing Load & FTE Compliance Checks

Audit Instructor Maximum Load Targets

Verify that teachers are not scheduled for more instruction blocks than their FTE allowance permits (e.g., standard 5 blocks max for secondary teachers). Over-assignment violates district contracts.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Reports → Teacher Section Load Audit
Tip: Keep a master worksheet of teacher prep blocks open on your screen and confirm that no instructor is assigned to teach during their designated prep periods.

Confirm Shared/Itinerant Travel Windows

Cross-examine schedules for shared split-site teachers (such as band or speech specialists) who split sites with adjacent schools. Confirm they are assigned to empty periods during travel windows.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Staff Assignments

Identify and Verify Staff Assignment placeholders

Identify all active sections currently assigned to temporary placeholder staff records (e.g., "STAFF MATH" or "STAFF SPANISH"). Cross-check these with outstanding credential clearances or recruiting pipelines.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → master schedule → Teacher Table
4

Student Request & Pre-Scheduling Audits

Isolate and Purge Duplicate Student Requests

Identify and delete duplicate course requests inside the SSS table (such as a student signed up for English 10 twice). Leaving duplicate requests breaks seat allocation math in student scheduling runs.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Student Requests → Request Reports
Isolating Query: LIST STU SSS STU.ID STU.LN STU.FN SSS.CN BY STU.ID IF SSS.CN = SSS.CN

Identify Students with Completely Empty Requests

Run a demographic check to isolate students who are currently missing required course requests. If students have blank SSS sheets, they cannot be placed during automated scheduling runs.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Reports → Student Request Audit

Verify Senior Graduation Pathway Alignments

Prioritize seniors (Grade 12) and check that required core graduation requirements are fully scheduled. Clear senior schedule gaps or unmatched requests first to ensure graduation pathway compliance.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Student Data → Graduation Status (REQ)
5

Run-Time Conflict & Validation Audits

Run a Pre-Scheduling Edit Listing Check

Execute the core Aeries pre-scheduling validation sweep to identify structural errors, missing teacher tags, invalid room IDs, and out-of-range periods before starting scheduling runs.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Reports → Prescheduling Edit Listing

Analyze the Scheduling Reject Analysis Listing (D7)

After executing your draft scheduling pass, analyze your Reject Code D7 listings. Check for recurring pattern errors: if a specific AP or elective is showing full, adjust the maximum seat capacity to balance scheduling matches.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Reports → Student Reject Analysis (D7)
Audit Target: Do not attempt to adjust individual students manually yet. Focus on correcting master schedule board conflicts first (such as overlapping singletons) to clear the bulk of your rejects automatically.

Acquire Principal Sign-Off and Freeze the Master Schedule

Confirm that your scheduling placement success rates meet WUSD targets (typically above 85% on your final run). Lock the verified Master Schedule draft in Aeries to freeze your configurations, preventing automated background runs from overwriting details.

Aeries Staging: Scheduling Process → Interactive Schedule Board → Lock Schedule
