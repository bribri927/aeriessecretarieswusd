🛠 Troubleshooting & Common Errors

Identify why schedules fail and how to fix common master scheduling problems.

Too many students unscheduled

Why it happens: Too few sections, singleton conflicts, missing alternates, required courses in too few periods, or teacher/room restrictions.

How to fix it: Review conflict reports, find patterns, add/move sections, adjust singleton placement, then re-run scheduling.

Teacher overloads

Why it happens: Duplicate sections, wrong terms, incorrect assignments, or too many sections assigned to one teacher.

How to fix it: Run teacher load reports and check period, term, prep count, and assigned sections.

Double-booked rooms

Why it happens: Room assignments were added after sections were created or shared rooms were not tracked.

How to fix it: Filter sections by room and period. Assign specialized spaces first.

Electives disappeared

Why it happens: Required courses or singletons blocked elective options, or elective priorities/alternates were incomplete.

How to fix it: Review request priorities and conflict reports before manually changing individual schedules.

Flex confusion

Why it happens: Flex purpose, attendance, assignment, and supervision processes were not defined before building schedules.

How to fix it: Document the flex operating model before implementation.

Prevention tip: Run validation checks weekly during scheduling season instead of waiting until schedules are “done.”
