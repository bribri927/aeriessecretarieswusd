Final Checklist

Final Master Schedule Submission Checklist

Use this checklist before turning in or publishing a master schedule. It is designed to prevent late, incomplete, or incorrect submissions.

Before You Submit

  • Bell schedule verified.
  • Instructional minutes reviewed.
  • Staffing/FTE confirmed.
  • Teacher loads reviewed.
  • Rooms assigned.
  • Shared staff conflicts reviewed.
  • SPED/ELD/intervention supports protected.
  • Flex/advisory/WIN process documented if used.

Student Schedule Checks

  • Course requests complete.
  • Missing requests reviewed.
  • Too few/too many requests reviewed.
  • Unscheduled students reviewed.
  • Conflicts reviewed and documented.
  • Graduation-critical students reviewed.
  • Counselor review completed where applicable.
  • Known exceptions documented.

Final Sign-Off

  • Principal reviewed the schedule.
  • Counselor/site team reviewed applicable student schedules.
  • Office staff understand schedule timelines and changes.
  • Reports were rerun after final edits.
  • Final version submitted according to district process.
Reminder: If you changed sections after a review, re-run the reports. Do not assume earlier validation is still accurate.
